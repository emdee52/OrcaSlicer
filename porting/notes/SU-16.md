# SU-16 - Support Zones: what does not fit the stumps falls plumb

- Source: `NEOTKOCM_RELEASE_2_47.md` ("Support that cannot reach a stump goes straight down").
- Fork: `OrcaFS-NeotkoCM` @ `3bd92365d0` (2.4.7). Base for this diff: Snapmaker `v2.4.0`.
- Category: B. Status: **ported** - build clean, `ctest` 2407/2407, print check pending.
- Marker: `[ORCAPORT:SU-16]`.

## The problem

Before, a part of a zone column that could not reach any stump - or that arrived wider than its
stump - was trimmed away against the stump band, and the support above it was left hanging above the
bed. The release note gives the measurements from two of the owner's slices: first 19 islands of the
tail reached no stump; after fixing only those, the bottom layer still lost `fuera_sin_v=53.97 mm2`
of 57.95. The column DID touch its stumps, but it arrived wide: the guided run is about two layers
(a 1 mm gap at 45 deg), with no time to contract, so the clip against the stump band ate everything
outside the stumps. On the bed the support ended at 2.6 mm, floating.

## The fix - per-AREA, not per-island

That is the whole insight: an island test cannot see "the column is wider than its stump", because
the island does touch. So the rule is applied by area, at two sites:

1. **At the stump cap** (`layer_id == zone_stump_top[zi]`): `diff(projection, all_stumps)` is handed
   to the general flow - `overhangs_projection`, and `enforcers_projection` too under
   build-plate-only (the base of the support comes from there, so it would be lost again) - and
   `projection` is clipped to `all_stumps`.
2. **Below the cap, layer by layer** (`layer_id < zone_stump_top[zi]`): the same against
   `corridor_here`. Measured on the third slice: the cap fell plumb 134 mm2 but the layer below lost
   another 43 (`fuera_sin_v=43.41`) - **new contact the stump footprint seeds just there**,
   expanded a little past the band by the grid, which the next layer's clip then ate.

What does land on a stump keeps using the stump. The pre-existing "no island reaches a stump"
warning is unchanged: it is the honest half of the gesture and still names the islands that missed
and how far short they fell.

## Anchor

One file, one function: `src/libslic3r/Support/SupportMaterial.cpp`, the per-zone per-layer corridor
loop. `projection` is `zones_projection[zi]` by reference, so truncating it persists to the lower
layers - which is what makes the hand-off stick. No new config key, no gesture change, no UI.
